# Requirements and source references

This folder contains the original source/reference material supplied for the HP 9835 unified RAM/ROM board design.

## Original archives
- `original_archives/RAM_Card.zip` — existing HP 9835 RAM expansion design and PAL/GAL source.
- `original_archives/DiagROM Single Card.zip` — diagnostic ROM/RAM design and DIAG ROM image.
- `original_archives/9835_ROMCard.zip` — existing HP 9835 option-ROM design and PAL/GAL source.

The same archives are unpacked under `extracted/` for convenient browsing. The archives themselves are preserved unchanged.

## Connector reference
- `fce_xx_sg_e_data_sheet.pdf` — 2.54 mm-pitch IDC card-edge socket drawing supplied as a mechanical reference.
- `reference_photos/connector_overview.png` — HP 9835 memory-bus connector photograph.
- `reference_photos/connector_pin_orientation.png` — photograph/reference used to establish edge-finger orientation: viewed from the component side with the fingers pointing upward, pin 2 is at the far right and pin 50 at the far left; odd pins are on the solder side and even pins on the component side.
