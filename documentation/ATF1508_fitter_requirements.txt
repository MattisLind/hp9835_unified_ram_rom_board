1. Use ATF1508AS, TQFP100 (not PQFP100), and the actual purchased speed grade.
2. Use -preassign KEEP; never silently relocate PCB pins when fitting fails.
3. Pin 35 / hp_mip_n: open collector; never push-pull HIGH.
   Fitter option: -str open_collector=hp_mip_n
   Rev G board net CPLD_MIP_N feeds U14/U13 hardware isolation before HP MIP/.
4. Disable keepers globally: -str pin_keep=OFF.
   Retain external switch pull-ups; verify MIP release behavior in hardware.
5. Enable JTAG: -str JTAG=ON. Fixed pads: TDI=4, TMS=15, TCK=62, TDO=73.
   Programming-interface decision revised 2026-11-07: order-time assembly choice
   of onboard FT232H USB-to-JTAG OR external JTAG header; do not populate both.
   External header: 2x5, 2.54mm, male THROUGH-HOLE, preferably keyed/shrouded,
   using the Atmel ATF15xx / ATDH1150USB JTAG-A pinout (section 15.2).
   Keep JTAG enabled in every fitted image for both assembly options.
   Rev G schematic includes both interfaces; see ../requirements/design_decisions.md,
   section 15, for signal mapping, isolation requirements and remaining work.
   USB-option bench power uses Schottky-isolated USB/HP sources (section 15.1).
   Check the purchased device's VCC/ISP limits after the diode drop.
   With USB power and HP off, keep HP bus drive disabled and MIP/ released.
   JP1: shunt fitted = RUN; open = PROGRAM. U12-U14 enforce isolation in hardware.
   Pin 36 / xcvr_oe_n is the request net CPLD_XCVR_OE_N, qualified by U14
   before reaching physical OE# pad 19 on U2/U3. This uses no extra CPLD ports.
6. Disable both power-down alternatives: -str pd1=OFF -str pd2=OFF.
   Pin 1 carries MEM_D0 and pin 41 carries MEM_OE_N.
7. Intended clocks: pin 87 HP_STM_N (raw STM/ from J3.42; falling edge), pin 90 HP_PH2.
   No external inverter is used; active-cycle gating tests STM/ LOW.
   Require the fitter to implement the falling-edge clock without changing pads.
8. Pin 41 MEM_OE_N and pin 42 MEM_WE_N are shared flash/SRAM strobes.
   Separate selects are FLASH_CE_N=40 and SRAM_CE_N=45.
9. Pin 89 is reserved/tied inactive; it is not a VHDL core port.
10. Inspect the fitted pin list and macrocell options, including unused-port
    handling for MEM_D15, FLASH_RYBY_N and constant FLASH_RESET_N.
    Verify normal MEM_D high impedance and EXTEST drive/capture behavior.
11. Require a successful resource/timing fit before freezing board routing.
